Medical Safety Information

This medication is a combination of two medicines: amoxicillin and clavulanic acid. Amoxicillin is an antibiotic belonging to the group of medicines called penicillins. It works by stopping the growth of bacteria.

Warning

If you develop a rash, swelling of the face, or difficulty breathing, seek immediate medical attention as these may be signs of a severe allergic reaction.
