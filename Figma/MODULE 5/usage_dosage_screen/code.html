Amoxicillin 500mg

Antibiotic • Capsule

Recommended Dose

Age Group Dosage
Adults 18+ years
500mg every 8 hours or 875mg every 12 hours
Children 3 - 17 years
20mg/kg/day in divided doses every 8 hours
Infants Under 3 months
Max 30mg/kg/day divided every 12 hours

Note: Dosages may vary based on infection severity and weight. Consult your doctor.

clinical_notes

How to Take

1

Take with or without food. Taking it with a meal can help reduce potential stomach upset.

2

Swallow the capsule whole with a full glass of water. Do not crush or chew the medication.

3

Finish the full course of treatment as prescribed, even if symptoms disappear after a few days.

We'll alert you 15 minutes before your next dose.

Keep medication stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
